# B9 ISO/IEC 27001:2022 NIST OLIR-derived technical alignment

This scoped technical alignment prototype uses ISO Open Data only for public
metadata for ISO/IEC 27001:2022 (edition 3, published 2022-10-25) and the
official NIST OLIR `800-53-Rev5-to-ISO 27001-2022 Informative Reference` v1.0.0
for relationships. ISO/IEC 27001:2022/Amd 1:2024 is recorded as published and
outside this network-device subset.

No ISO publication/PDF, normative prose, Annex A text, or ISO/IEC 27002 text
was ingested, stored, or redistributed. The NIST OLIR is an informative
crosswalk: its mappings do not establish equivalence, ISO conformity, or
certification. B9 reuses bounded B6 technical conditions across Cisco IOS XE
17, FortiOS 7, and Junos 18 where canonical evidence exists.

This is not ISO/IEC 27001 compliance, a complete conformity assessment,
certification readiness, ISO certification, or an ISO-approved mapping.
